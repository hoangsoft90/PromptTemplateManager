# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Prompt Template Manager** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app on your mobile device.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Overview

Prompt Template Manager is a **personal prompt template library** app. Its core purpose is to let you store, search, fill in, and copy reusable AI prompts.

The app is designed to be **100% on-device**:

- **No account.** You do not need to sign up or log in.
- **No backend.** There is no server, database, or cloud storage operated by us.
- **No cloud sync.** Your data never leaves your device except for actions you explicitly initiate (see Section 4).

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves.**

The app has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting backend, and no telemetry. We have no ability to see, access, or retrieve the prompts, titles, or any other content you store in the app.

### Data stored on your device

All of the data you create lives **only on your device** in the app's local database:

- Your prompt templates (title, content, category, tags)
- Usage history (how often and when a template was used)
- Favorites and app preferences (e.g., whether a one-time tip or onboarding step has been shown)

This data is used solely to make the app work — e.g., to show your library, power search, and rank recently used templates. It is never sent to us or to any third party (with the single exception described in Section 4).

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by **Google AdMob** (`react-native-google-mobile-ads`).

- The app requests **non-personalized ads only** (`requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true`). This means ads are **not** targeted to you based on your browsing history, interests, or profile.
- AdMob, as a third party, may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / iOS Advertising Identifier, IP address, and general device information) to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and limit how often you see an ad. How Google processes this data is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob's policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- In certain regions (e.g., EEA, UK), you may be shown a consent screen provided by Google before ads are served.
- Because the app uses non-personalized ads, it does **not** use your data for personalized advertising and does **not** share personal data with advertisers for targeting purposes.

To learn more about advertising identifiers and how to control them:

- **Android:** Google Play settings → Ads, or the device's advertising ID settings ("Delete advertising ID" / "Reset advertising ID").
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and "Limit Ad Tracking".

## 4. Import and Export

The app lets you back up your library to a **JSON file** and restore from one.

- **Export:** When you tap "Export", the app writes a JSON file to your device's storage (or shares it via the system share sheet). The content of that file is your prompt library. Where you send or save that file is entirely your choice.
- **Import:** When you import a JSON file, the file is read directly from your device. On the web version, the file is read locally in your browser and is not uploaded to any server.

These actions are always user-initiated and never automatic.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, there is no children's data for us to collect.

## 6. Data Security

Since your data never leaves your device, there is no data to secure in transit or at rest on our side. The security of the data on your device is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend enabling your device's built-in security features to protect your on-device data.
